# Print Swap: garment types and part names

The garment types A to H that step 2 offers, the parts step 6 looks for, and the
trims each type protects. Types I, J … are the ones your team saves with
**Other > Save as type** (see the guide, section 3d).

## Naming rules

1. ALL CAPS, 2 to 3 words.
2. Pattern: SIDE + AREA + DETAIL, e.g. LEFT HAND TRIM. Leave out what does not apply
   (CHEST POCKET, NECK BAND).
3. LEFT and RIGHT are the VIEWER's left and right (the team's rule).
4. Same word for the same thing on every garment:
   - HAND = sleeve (team word). TRIM = band or cuff at an edge. INSIDE = inside or back of a
     piece seen through an opening.
   - TOP = upper body of a top. PANT = pant leg. SHORT = short leg. GOWN = nightgown body.
   - FRONT / BACK only when the body is one piece, front or back view.
5. Protected trims (kept on top, recolored, not printed): PIPING, BUTTONS, LACE, RUFFLE LACE,
   DRAWCORD, BOW, ZIPPER.
6. One-word team names are kept only where they are already in the team's file (TOP, PANT,
   POCKET).

## A. Notch collar pajama set, long sleeve, piped (the team's style)

Type A uses the team's own names from their print swap file, in their order. The team's
"LEFT PATN TRIM / RIGHT PATN TRIM" is spelled **LEFT PANT TRIM / RIGHT PANT TRIM**.

| Part | What it is |
|---|---|
| LEFT TOP / RIGHT TOP | Front panels, split at the placket |
| TOP | The rest of the shirt (back, and any yoke showing) |
| LEFT COLLAR / RIGHT COLLAR | Collar and lapel each side |
| RIGHT NECK | Inside back neck behind the collar (LEFT NECK only when it shows in two pieces) |
| POCKET | Chest pocket |
| POCKET UPPER | Pocket top band |
| LEFT HAND / RIGHT HAND | Sleeves |
| LEFT HAND TRIM / RIGHT HAND TRIM | Sleeve cuffs |
| RIGHT HAND TRIM INSIDE | Inside of the cuff opening |
| LEFT PANT / RIGHT PANT | Pant legs |
| PANT | The rest of the pants (waistband and center) |
| LEFT PANT TRIM / RIGHT PANT TRIM | Pant hem bands |
| LEFT INSIDE | Back of the leg showing on the front |
| Protected | PIPING, BUTTONS |

Proposed tidy-ups of the team's names (not applied; the build uses the names above):

| Team name | Proposed | Why |
|---|---|---|
| POCKET UPPER | POCKET TRIM | same TRIM word as cuffs and hems |
| RIGHT NECK | BACK NECK | it is the inside back neck, not a side |
| LEFT INSIDE | LEFT PANT INSIDE | says which piece it belongs to |
| RIGHT HAND TRIM INSIDE | RIGHT HAND INSIDE | 3 words max |
| TOP (rest of shirt) | BACK TOP | says what it is |
| PANT (rest of pant) | PANT CENTER | says what it is |
